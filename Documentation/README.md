# Documentation

This folder contains the supporting documentation for AI Insight Sprint™ Case Study #2 — Nigeria Tax Revenue & Compliance Intelligence.

## Case Study Objective

To demonstrate how taxpayer, assessment and payment data can be transformed into actionable revenue, compliance and executive decision intelligence.

## AI Insight Sprint™ Framework

The analysis follows:

**Evidence → Insight → Action → Decision**

## Key Analytical Areas

### 1. Revenue Intelligence
Analysis of assessed revenue, recorded payments, payment ratios and outstanding exposure.

### 2. Tax Compliance Intelligence
Analysis of payment status, no-payment assessments, zero-value payments, payment timing and overpayment signals.

### 3. Taxpayer & Geographic Intelligence
Analysis of taxpayer segments, revenue concentration, states and leading LGAs.

### 4. Executive Decision Intelligence
Translation of validated findings into management priorities, recommended actions and risk considerations.

## Key Findings

- Approximately ₦14.13B in assessed revenue.
- Approximately ₦5.66B in recorded payments.
- Payment-to-assessment ratio of 40.03%.
- Approximately ₦9.42B in positive outstanding exposure.
- 11,242 assessment records with no payment record.
- Large taxpayers represent 14.98% of the taxpayer base and 33.83% of assessed revenue.
- Ogun has the highest state-level outstanding exposure rate at 66.31%, while Lagos has the highest assessed revenue at approximately ₦2.74B.

## Important Analytical Considerations

Payment anomalies were treated as signals requiring investigation rather than automatically removed.

In particular:

- No payment record does not automatically prove non-compliance.
- Zero-value payments are distinct from missing payment records.
- Overpayments are reconciliation signals and should not automatically be treated as additional revenue.
- Pre-assessment payments may reflect legitimate prepayments, posting or adjustment processes, or data-quality issues.

> **Data Note:** This case study uses a simulated tax administration dataset created for analytical demonstration and portfolio purposes. It does not represent official Nigerian government revenue records.
